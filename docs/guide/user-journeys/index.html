Arya user journeys

Seven journeys, from the first receipt to a shared household. Each one has a person, a goal, the screens in order, and sample data. The numbers come from the demo household (python -m harness.make_demo_household data), so you can open the live demo and see the same thing.

Store names: these journeys use real store names from my own shopping. The hosted demo (Demo Town) uses invented ones, such as Costcozy and Trader Jill's.

The list comes first. The Quest hunt is optional.

The demo household. The fictional Sharma family. 39 receipts from May 2 to Oct 24, 2026: Trader Joe's 18, Costco 12, Safeway 9. Total spend is $1,115.42. That is $1,090.42 of groceries, plus a $25.00 gift card (bought for someone else). One chicken was returned for $12.30. The demo slides all dates to the current week. The screenshots were taken on Oct 29, 2026, so a date such as "Oct 19" in a picture is a seed date moved forward. Money amounts never move.

Where prices come from. Every price in this page comes from the household's own receipts. Arya does not scrape stores, read flyers or search the web for prices, and it calls no store API for prices. (Google Places finds stores for the store picker, and Kroger's API gives aisle hints; neither supplies prices.)

Screenshots are real 390x844 captures of the demo, in assistant/docs/img/.

Journey 1. First receipt, and the list starts to write itself​

Person. Priya Sharma. She shops every Saturday. She writes lists in her head and forgets milk.

Goal. Put in last week's receipts and see what Arya learns.

Status. Shipped. Reading the photo or PDF needs a model.

StepWhat Priya doesWhat Arya shows
1Opens the app, taps Try it now or creates an account.Landing: "Your grocery run, smarter." Three real rows (Whole Milk "Running low").
2Lists, Receipts (the group at the bottom), Add receipts. She picks the Costco PDF from Oct 17. More than one file is fine.One progress line per file, then one summary: filed, already filed, could not read.
3Waits a few seconds.Muse Spark reads the lines. Code adds them up. The receipt shows a check mark and its total only if the lines match the printed total.
4Files the same PDF again by mistake."Already filed." Arya compares a fingerprint (store, date, lines, total) and does not count the trip twice.
5Wants one old receipt. In Receipts, types the store in the search box, opens Date and picks a range, and picks Store A to Z or Highest total in Sort.A line shows how many receipts match and their total. Clear removes the filters.
6Spots a misread line on a filed receipt. Opens it, taps the line, and changes the name, quantity or price in Fix this line.The line shows an "edited" tag. A new name also fixes the item on her other receipts. The printed total never changes. If the lines stop adding up, the receipt says so and no longer counts as checked. Undo puts the old values back. In a group, any member can fix a line, but only the person who filed the receipt can delete it.
7Opens Today.Running low lists items that are due.

Sample data: the Costco receipt, Oct 17

LinePrice
Pasture Eggs 12 ct$6.47
Jasmine Rice 10 lb$13.49
Paper Towels 6 ct$9.99
AA Batteries 8 ct$8.99
Kirkland Organic Milk 0.5 gal$4.14
Total$43.08 (the lines add up, so it posts)

What she learns on Today. After 26 Saturdays the pattern for Whole Milk is clear: "Every ~7 days; last bought 10 days ago", window Oct 24 to Oct 27. Sourdough Loaf has the same line. Each row has an Add button.

The rules behind it

  • A pattern needs 3 kept buys before Arya calls an item "running low".
  • Returns count as not bought. The returned chicken (Oct 5, $12.30) never comes back as a suggestion.
  • Bad lines go to a review queue. Nothing posts in silence.
  • The gift card is tracked under "Bought for others". It is never suggested.

Journey 2. Plan a Costco and Trader Joe's trip​

Person. Priya, on Friday evening.

Goal. One list per store, with the usual things already there.

Status. Shipped. Typing and tapping are code. Voice and photo adds need a model.

Update 2026-11-02: "Everything else" is no longer an automatic list (it exists only if you make one), and Lists now sits under More. The steps below and their screenshot show the earlier layout and are due a re-walk.

The why sheet for Whole Milk shows Arya's reasons in plain words: "You buy it every ~7 days. 25 times since May." "Last bought 10 days ago. That is 3 days past your usual." Price lines follow in Journey 4.

Also on this screen

  • Typing "milk" finds "Whole Milk". Typing a non-grocery item, for example "light bulbs", keeps it under Other errands.
  • Items you add are not changed without your tap. Riskier changes (out of stock, never again, snooze) ask first.
  • Planned: none for this journey.

Journey 3. In the store with Quest​

Person. Priya, Saturday at Trader Joe's.

Goal. Get everything on the list without walking back.

Status. Shipped. A quest needs no model. "Find it for me" needs one. Express, the plain-checklist mode, no longer has an entry point. Its screen stays only for runs started before it was removed.

StepWhat Priya doesWhat Arya shows
1Opens the Trader Joe's list. Taps Start shopping · 3 items.A first-time how-to: Find it, Grab it, Snap the receipt. A list with no items sends her to Add items instead.
2Taps Got it."Trader Joe's · Quest", a points counter, "0 of 3". The store's layout is labelled as a typical layout until shoppers map it. A card says "Pasta 1 lb x2, Pasta Shelf".
3Taps Grab.+10 points. She can put an item back.
4An item is not on the shelf. She taps Not here? and confirms.The report is shared. Once three different shoppers have reported the same item at a store, other shoppers who have it on their list and use that store see a "3 shoppers say not here" alert on Today.
5Taps Find it for me and aims the camera at a shelf.SAM 3.1 boxes candidates, Muse Spark checks each one, and the match lights up for one tap.
6Taps I'm done shopping, then photographs the receipt.+50 points. The receipt is filed with the same checks as Journey 1.

Rules. No clock. No lives. No way to lose. Quest is optional. The list works without it.

Sample data. "Trader Joe's · Quest", 3 items on the card: Avocados 4 ct, Pasta 1 lb x2, Hummus.

Journey 4. Compare prices from my own receipts​

Person. Arjun Sharma, who pays the bills and wants to spend less.

Goal. Find out where milk is cheaper, and what has gone up.

Status. Shipped. Arya uses only the household's own receipts. Community prices, Deals and Arya Shoppers are Planned. Today the opt-in choice is saved and nothing else is built. There are no price charts. Trends show as words and percent.

Arya's powers. Settings, About Arya, has a page that lists what Arya already does and what turns on next, with one line per power. One "Did you know?" line shows on Today each day.

Limits. Arya says "your prices" and never "the store's price". With no receipt for an item at a store, it makes no claim for that store. Spark never finds or guesses a price.

Journey 5. A household with Groups​

People. Priya and her partner Arjun. Each has their own account on their own phone.

Goal. One shared set of lists and receipts. A clean way out.

Status. Shipped, on by default in docker-compose.yml (ARYA_GROUPS=1, PR #128). Set ARYA_GROUPS=0 in deploy/.env to turn it off. The demo household cannot join a group, so this journey needs two real accounts. It needs a deploy before the live site has it. Live sync uses Postgres LISTEN/NOTIFY when DATABASE_URL is set. It has not been tried against the Supabase session pooler. If the pooler refuses it, set ARYA_LIVE_PG=0.

StepWhoWhat happens
1PriyaSettings, Start a group, names it "Sharma home". Her lists become the group's lists. She is the owner.
2PriyaTaps Invite someone. Arya shows a 6-character code, for example K7M2QX. It lasts 48 hours. Any member can make one. A group holds up to 6 people.
3ArjunOpens Join a group, types the code. Arya shows a preview first: who invited, how many people, how many lists.
4ArjunJoins. His lists merge into the group's. Lists with the same name become one list. Both people now see Costco and Trader Joe's.
5ArjunAdds Hummus to Trader Joe's in the aisle.
6PriyaHer open list reloads in about a second. A tab skips the echo of its own change.
7BothReceipts from both accounts show in Receipts, tagged with the person who filed each one.

Leaving

StepWhat happens
1Arjun taps Leave group. The sheet says: "You keep a copy of the lists and your own receipts."
2He taps Leave group again.
DataAfter he leaves
ListsDuplicated. Arjun has a copy. The group keeps its own.
ReceiptsBelong to the account that filed them. Each goes with its owner.
Running low, patterns, spendRecomputed for both sides from the receipts each side now has.
Personal items (profile, badges, Quest, push, currency)Never shared, so nothing changes.

If the owner leaves, the member who joined first becomes owner. When the last member leaves, the group is gone.

Not planned. Roles, per-list privacy, kids' accounts.

Journey 6. Expiry dates and Use soon​

Person. Priya, unpacking the Saturday shop.

Goal. Use the spinach before it turns.

Status. Shipped. Reading a date from a photo needs a model. In the demo, a canned sample read stands in for the photo.

StepWhat Priya doesWhat Arya shows
1Taps + Snap a date on Today, then photographs "USE BY OCT 31" on the spinach.Spark reads the date. Arya shows what it read: "Baby Spinach 16 oz, use by Oct 31".
2Taps Looks right, track it.Nothing is saved until she confirms. The photo is never kept.
3Looks at Today three days later.Use soon shows Baby Spinach 16 oz with the days left. It shows items up to 7 days ahead. A past date stays for 7 days, then fades.
4Taps the row in Use soon.She can snooze it (it waits 2 days), fix the date or stop tracking.
5Buys spinach again.A new receipt means a new package, so the old date drops.

Still good? Arya asks by itself when an item "usually turns about now". The demo shows Still good? · 3 to check: Baby Spinach 16 oz, Chicken Breast and 1 more. Priya taps Still good or It expired. This part is code, not a model.

Rule for a past date. A past date is neutral ("your call"), not a red alarm.

Journey 7. Ask in the aisle, a reminder, and the Home Screen​

Person. Priya, in the week.

Goal. Get answers without opening a spreadsheet, and get a nudge before milk runs out.

7a. Ask Arya​

Status. Shipped. Needs a model. Without MODEL_API_KEY the Ask tab says it could not answer.

In the Ask tab Priya can type, or tap Talk to speak. Arya answers in text only:

She saysArya does
"We finished the Greek yogurt."Applies the change and the list updates.
"What do we spend on coffee?"Answers from the ledger (Coffee Beans 12 oz, $104.80 over 26 weeks in the demo). It does not invent a number.
"Where is milk cheaper?"Reads the price facts that code computed from her receipts (see Journey 4). The model only reads them.
"What is Arya?"Answers about Arya itself.
A photo (Photo chip under the Ask field)Opens a chooser over the existing readers (list, receipt, fridge, expiry date), or "Ask about this photo" once. The photo is not stored.

During a hunt a bar "Ask Arya..." sits at the bottom. It opens a sheet with three quick questions: "Where's the {item}?", "Is there a cheaper one?", "I can't find it". The sheet knows the store, the item on screen and what is left, so "this" and "it" work.

7b. Push reminder​

Status. Shipped, live on the hosted app (flag ARYA_PUSH_REMINDERS=1, VAPID keys, daily cron at 13:00 UTC; enabled Nov 2). Setup is in assistant/docs/PUSH-REMINDERS.md. Demo accounts never see it.

  1. Settings, Preferences, Remind me. Priya turns it on and picks the notice window (1 to 7 days, default 2).
  2. At 9:00 the daily job runs. It sends at most one push per account per day. No model call. $0.
  3. Example push, one item: "Whole Milk 1 gal is probably running out" with "Add it to this week's list? Tap to open your list."
  4. Example push, several: "3 things are about to run out" with "Whole Milk 1 gal, Sourdough Loaf, Pasture Eggs 12 ct. Tap to see them."
  5. A cycle is announced once. A new receipt starts a new cycle.

On iPhone, reminders work only after Add to Home Screen (iOS 16.4 or newer).

7c. Add Arya to the Home Screen​

Status. Shipped (PR #133). Hidden in the demo and once Arya runs from the Home Screen.

PhoneWhat happens
Android and desktop ChromeA card at the top of Today: Add Arya to your Home Screen. Full screen, with reminders. The Install button runs the browser's own prompt.
iPhoneThe card says Show how. It opens 4 steps, including "leave Open as Web App on". Off means a plain Safari tab, and then push cannot work.

The card shows from the second visit, only when the list has an item. If Priya taps the cross, it stays away for 30 days. Settings always has an Add to Home Screen row while installing is possible.
