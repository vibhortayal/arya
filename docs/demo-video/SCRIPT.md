# Arya demo video script (working copy)

Status: owner draft of 2026-11-03, not locked. Spoken text is the owner's, verbatim. Text in (parentheses) is a stage direction, not spoken.

Grocery shopping is an integral part of our lives. Hunters and gatherers did it to survive. Think about what it takes today. You have many lists in your notes, your partner sends you items over text, while they are shopping at another store. So both of you bought yogurt, and neither bought the toilet paper that ran out. The milk your kids need is out of stock, so now you need to go to another store. You hold all of it in your head, and it's still never quite right.

I built Arya for this hackathon to make the whole grocery routine easier. My goal is to put the whole chore in one place — shared lists, a guided store route, visibility into your spend and usage patterns, and out-of-stock warnings from other shoppers so your trip isn't wasted. If we could share what we see — where things are, what's good, what's cheaper — nobody starts from zero.Humans helping Humans. This is Arya.

I didn't want a chatbot bolted onto a grocery list, so I started with the hard part: the learning engine. I used Muse Wonder to write the first version of Arya engine to scan receipts to feed the lists. I downloaded my receipts from the Costco app — my own shopping data.

Arya showed me my habits. Egg whites in 35 of 114 trips. And the same bag of almond flour: $13.59 at one Costco, $16.99 at another.( shows on screen)

I wanted to build a complete end to end product. My shopping list, generated by my usage patterns, and spend analysis using my receipts. And something to bring joy to my shopping chore - "Quest" mode. ( shows on screen).
(at home,screen is shown from on) The list is already made from what we buy. I add by typing or by voice or a photo of a paper list - powered by Meta AI models. And it already told me that eggs are out of stock even before I started my trip. (in store) Arya walks me from item to item in quest mode. I point the camera at a shelf: SAM finds the item, Spark checks it. If an item is out of stock , I tap "Not here", and the next shopper knows. At checkout I snap the receipt. Spark reads it, and if anything is unreadable, Muse Wonder takes a second look. Next week's list is already learning.

(at home now) Arya tells me that the spinach is about to go bad. And I just finished the yogurt, so I tell Arya we're out, and it's on the list.

Arya is live — the demo is open to everyone, no sign-up; accounts are by invite from the waitlist for now, since every receipt costs money to read. I also integrated Meta Muse AI agent over MCP to talk to Arya. A developer portal is live for API integration. Honestly, it's early: a week is not enough to build all of this, and it gets better with community use. The potential is bigger.
