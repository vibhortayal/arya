Model policy: cost efficiency (my directive, 2026-10-27)

Rule: every job runs on the cheapest model that holds its golden-set score. Escalation is by evidence (a scored eval), never by default.

Prices (per 1M tokens, Meta Model API, verified 2026-10-27)​

  • muse-spark-1.3 Standard: $1.25 in / $0.15 cached / $4.25 out
  • muse-spark-1.3 Contributor: $0.10 in / $0.002 cached / $0.20 out (different data terms; not used). Limit 150 requests/min. Not the app's tier; see "Spark tier" below.
  • muse-wonder-1.0: $4.00 in / $0.20 cached / $20.00 out. This is the expensive frontier model, not a budget tier. It is banned from volume paths.
  • muse-image-1.0: $0.01/image. muse-voice-transcribe-1.0: $0.18/audio-hour.
  • sam-3.1: $2.50 per 1,000 images; $0.20 per 1,000 video frames.

Assignments​

  1. The cheapest model is no model. Patterns, cadence, run-out dates, ledger math, reconciliation, and list generation are deterministic code: $0.
  2. Volume intelligence (receipt decode fallback, list and date photos, command parsing, Ask Arya, judge): Spark, reasoning_effort=low, stable prefixes cached. Spark runs as muse-spark-1.3 Standard (see "Spark tier" below). Exception (owner, 2026-11-04): the list photo read sends reasoning_effort=minimal for speed, and repeats once at low if the API refuses the value.
  3. Eval loops (golden sets, prompt variants): Contributor mode. Gate scores still count on Standard. (Superseded for app traffic by the 2026-11-01 amendment.)
  4. Wonder: zero jobs at baseline (history; the live reader is auto, see below). A job earns Wonder only by failing its golden set on Spark at two effort levels, with the eval logged.
  5. Voice: transcribe on demand only; Ask has no voice out (replies are text).
  6. Muse Image: painted the 23 characters once and paints share looks (live); a dinner render is planned, not built. muse-image-1.0 costs $0.01 per image. It is the one place a flat per-unit price beats token metering.

Measured anchors (default reasoning effort, spike runs)​

  • PDF receipt decode: ~2.9 cents/receipt. Photo: ~1.5 cents short, ~3.1 long.
  • JSON import: $0. Patterns on imported history: $0.
  • Policy targets: a full trip decode at or under 1 to 2 cents; total hackathon API spend well under the $150 credit (plan §8 estimated $57 to $61 at defaults).

Credits (me, 2026-10-29)​

There is no $0 rule. Hackathon credits (Meta Model API, plus Baseten and Modal) are available, so spending on Meta models (Muse Image, Spark, Wonder, SAM) is allowed when it improves the product or demo. Stay budget-aware: the cheapest model that holds its score is still the default, spend goes through the ledger, and large batches get a capped trial first. The in-code caps (daily and demo budgets) are unchanged.

Hackathon amendment (2026-10-27, per WONDER-EVAL.md; changed 2026-11-01)​

My decision 2026-11-01: receipt photos are read by the auto reader (Ops "Photo reader" = "Spark first, Wonder as backup"): Spark reads first. Wonder re-reads only when Spark fails or the checks below flag the read (arithmetic, low confidence on too many lines). Wonder stays allowed as that second reader through Nov 9. That is meant to cover judging after submission. The original 2026-10-27 amendment (Wonder first, Spark fallback) is summarized below.

Original (2026-10-27): through Nov 4, judge-facing photo decode ran Wonder first with a Spark fallback. Wonder scored 97 of 98 lines after hand adjudication (the automatic scorer gives Wonder 86 of 98) against Spark's 92 of 98 on the automatic scorer. The numbers, latency, cost and the staple-judge check are in WONDER-EVAL.md.

Auto reader (built 2026-11-01; my pick for judging on 2026-11-01; NOT yet measured on photos). ARYA_PHOTO_READER=auto (or the Ops "Photo reader" switch) reads with Spark first and runs the checks in api/decode/checks.py (lines add up to the subtotal, subtotal plus tax equals the total, the footer item count, impossible prices, too many unsure lines, a price far from the same item number's earlier price on the account). Only a receipt that fails them is re-read by Wonder. A Spark failure goes straight to Wonder. If Wonder is down, the Spark read is kept and every line is sent to review. The receipt records decoded_by and second_read (the reasons), so it is never silent. Why it exists: Wonder won the eval on accuracy. Spark is about nine times cheaper per receipt and about four times faster. Cheap arithmetic can catch most of Spark's mistakes. Known gap, from the Gate 0 notes: a price rotation (two prices swapped between lines) keeps the sum, so arithmetic alone misses it. The price history check covers it only for item numbers seen before. Before the default changes, run python3 -m harness.photo_reader_eval --photos DIR on the Gate 0 photos and require that every price where Spark and Wonder differ is on a flagged receipt. After Nov 9 the amendment above ends. If I extend it, auto keeps Wonder's accuracy only where the checks say it is needed. Otherwise photo decode is Spark only.

Demo budget. Each demo visit may spend ARYA_DEMO_SESSION_BUDGET_USD (code default $0.40; $2 on the live site, owner 2026-11-04) on live reads, held in that visitor's in-memory sandbox; all demo visits together stay under ARYA_DEMO_DAILY_BUDGET_USD (code default $2; $100 a day on the live site for the judging window, about 50 full visits a day, and the top of the Ops range). Ops > Settings > Dollar limits sets both without a deploy and beats the env file. To go back through the env file, set ARYA_DEMO_DAILY_BUDGET_USD=2 and ARYA_DEMO_SESSION_BUDGET_USD=0.40 in the VM env file, then run docker compose up -d in assistant/deploy (a plain restart does not re-read the env file).

Switch after Nov 9, not before. The photo reader is an env setting: ARYA_PHOTO_READER=spark in the VM env file, then docker compose up -d in assistant/deploy (not restart, which keeps the old environment), puts photo decode on Spark Standard with no deploy. Unset, or wonder, is the amendment path. Receipts record decoded_by, so the switch is visible in the data. Spark-only photo decode scored 93.88% in the Gate 0 eval, with the review queue as the mitigation.

Spark tier (my decision, 2026-11-01)​

Spark and Wonder run in Standard mode. Meta's models page (dev.meta.ai/docs/models): "In Standard mode, your content is not used to train Meta models." Earlier on 2026-11-01 I had moved the app's Spark calls to muse-spark-1.3-contributor for the lower price; the same day I moved them back to Standard, so nothing the app sends is used for training.

  • Code: meta_client.resolve_mode() maps the app's "standard" (Spark) mode to the tier set on the Ops screen or in ARYA_SPARK_TIER; the Ops screen setting wins. Wonder is always Standard. An explicit mode="contributor" with personal=True is still refused.
  • Option, not built: a community (Contributor) tier with user opt-in could support more users at lower cost. It needs an opt-in screen, a per-user routing rule and updated privacy text first, and my word.
  • Rate limits: Standard allows 3,000 requests/min per team. The Contributor tier allows 150/min, so meta_client keeps a sliding-window gate at 140/min for that case. A call that would wait over 20 s fails with "Arya's AI is busy right now" (HTTP 502) instead of hanging.
  • Receipt photos follow the auto reader above. SAM, Muse Voice and the open-weights client are unchanged.
