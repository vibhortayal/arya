Staple-judge evaluation, 2026-10-27, on my own 114 Costco receipts (shared with my approval). The raw receipts and state files are not in this repo.

Staple-judge accuracy: one defensible number

In three lines.

  1. Of the 52 items the staple judge admitted to the list on my 114 receipts, 41 (79%) are staples by purchase cadence alone. No human labels, no model calls, $0.
  2. The 11 others are mostly slow-cycle items bought 3 times; one is a clear error.
  3. The figure moves with the proxy's definition (0.98 with a looser bar, 0.63 with a stricter one), so quote it with the definition, for one household.

Headline: precision 0.79 (41 of 52). One-sentence definition the submission may quote: "Of the 52 items Arya's staple judge admitted to the self-writing list, 41 (79%) were independently corroborated as behavioral staples by purchase cadence alone (bought on at least 4 separate receipts spanning at least 3 different months, returns netted), with no human labels involved."

Quotable form: "Judge precision 79% against a cadence-derived ground truth (41/52 admits corroborated); the 11 uncorroborated admits are dominated by slow-cycle consumables bought 3 times, and only one admit (Reynolds Foil, 2 buys 703 days apart) is a clear error."

Method (stated before the numbers)​

  • Data. My live state, copied read-only from the Oracle VM (Docker volume arya-data, per-user dir): 114 real Costco receipts, 2023-10-25 to 2026-10-26; the dictionary (458 entries); and judged.json, the judge's cached verdicts. No model calls were made; spend $0.
  • Identical pipeline. The analysis re-ran the app's own code on the copies: ledger build (discounts folded, fee SKUs excluded), dictionary grouping (Curator merges applied), and the pattern engine's returns netting (FIFO, kept quantity only, FOR-OTHERS lines excluded). Item identities are therefore exactly the identities the judge saw.
  • Admitted set. Items whose cached verdict has list_ok = true (class STAPLE, or SEASONAL). 156 raw verdicts collapse to 155 distinct grouped items under the current group map (one Curator merge); 52 of them are admitted (43 STAPLE, 9 SEASONAL).
  • Ground-truth proxy (behavioral staples). An item is a proxy staple if, after returns netting, it was bought on ≥ K distinct receipts spanning ≥ M distinct calendar months. Primary setting K=4, M=3. Justification: the corpus holds ~3 receipts/month over 36 months, so 4 separate trips in 3 different months is the weakest pattern that excludes one-trip stock-ups, gift/event buys, and two-trip trials, while still catching a quarterly Costco rhythm. The judge's own eligibility bar is 2 buys, so the proxy is deliberately stricter than the judge: it corroborates the judge's rule but does not re-derive it.
  • Metrics. Precision = admitted ∩ proxy / admitted. Recall = admitted ∩ proxy / proxy. Disagreements are listed by name below; the names matter more than the decimals.
  • What this number is NOT. It is not accuracy against a hand-labeled gold set, because no such set exists for this household. It measures agreement between a reasoning judge and an independent cadence proxy. Where they disagree, either can be right. The disagreement lists say which, case by case.

Disagreements at the primary setting​

Judge admitted, proxy does not corroborate (11 of 52)​

Ten of the eleven miss the bar by exactly one receipt or one month. All eleven are slow-cycle or short-window items where the judge's stated reason is plausible on its face:

  • Three buys, three months (7): Avocados; Hand Wash; Kirkland Signature Bodywash; Kirkland Signature Pink Salt; Lubriderm; Organic 2% Milk; Power Up Mega Omega Trail Mix. Soap, lotion, salt and foil are bought rarely because they last; a cadence bar built for food under-counts them. Avocados (Nov 2023 to Jan 2024, then nothing) is the weakest of this group in hindsight.
  • Four buys inside two months (3): Kirkland Signature Butter Quarters, Kirkland Signature Organic Whole Milk, Mini Cukes: a real rhythm of about once every two weeks in Nov and Dec 2023 that does not reappear under the same SKUs later in the corpus (bought elsewhere, or re-SKU'd).
  • The one clear error (1): Reynolds Foil (printed "Reynlds Foil" on the receipt): 2 buys, 703 days apart, judged STAPLE ("long-life foil rebought"). It is also the only uncorroborated admit under the looser K=3, M=2 proxy.

Proxy staple, judge kept off (14 of 55)​

All fourteen carry the class OCCASIONAL, and every judge reason cites gap irregularity: Buter Croiss (5 receipts), Cantaloupe (6), KS Lightly Breaded Chicken Breast Chunks (5), KS Pine Nuts (4), KS Pistachio (5), KS Towel (5), KS Walnuts (6), Mixed Pepper (4), Nmcholstoff+ (4), Organic Broc 2Lb (4), Organic Brussels (4), Organic Gre Bean (4), Pumpkin Seed (5), Red Onions (4). None were left unjudged. The judge saw all of them and declined. This is the product's stated policy made visible (precision over recall: a missed item costs the user one tap, an unnecessary suggestion costs trust). Twice-a-year pantry goods (walnuts, pistachios, pine nuts, pumpkin seeds) are the arguable cases: a human might call them staples; the judge calls a 150 to 250 day median gap "occasional," and under its own prompt that is a defensible call.

Second signal: my behavior​

  • Flags: my state has no flags.json. I have recorded no list edits (no "never suggest", no dismissals, no manual adds) against the judge's output. There is therefore no me-vs-judge settlement signal to report; absence of overrides is noted, not scored.
  • The live list: the cart prepared from the current smart list holds 5 items. The 4 pattern-surfaced items (Organic Raspberry, Mandarins, Kirkland Signature Almonds, Slice Almond, the list that went from 16 to 4 in the first judge sweep) are all corroborated proxy staples at the primary setting (4/4). The fifth, Power Up Mega Omega Trail Mix, is judge-admitted (STAPLE, 3 buys at a 23-day median gap) and sits in the proxy-miss group above by one month of spread. My behavior (it is on my active list, qty 2) sides with the judge.

Sensitivity and how to read the headline​

The precision figure depends on the definition, by construction. Loosen the proxy to K=3, M=2 and precision rises to 0.98, with recall falling to 0.58 (the judge is far more selective than a loose cadence bar). Tighten it to K=6, M=4 and precision falls to 0.63, because a heavier proxy excludes mid-frequency items the judge reasonably admits. K=4, M=3 was fixed before the results were seen, as the weakest bar that still excludes stock-ups and trials; 0.79 is quoted with that definition attached, never bare.

Caveats​

  • One household, one retailer (Costco), 114 receipts. Staples bought at other stores are invisible to both the judge and the proxy.
  • Verdicts are cached at the buy-count current when judged (re-judging triggers at +2 buys); the proxy uses final corpus counts. A verdict can therefore predate an item's later purchases. This cuts against the judge in the recall direction only.
  • Grouping is the Curator's current grouping; a wrong merge would move both sets together, so grouping errors mostly cancel in precision.
  • SEASONAL admits are month-gated in the live list logic; this eval scores the judge's list_ok, which is the judge's own decision, not the seasonal gate's.

Analysis: analysis script and state copies are kept out of the repo (my data).
