Fun pass — the cast is the game

Proposal (owner direction, 2026-10-28). Diagnosis: the app already owns a cast of 20 Muse Image food characters — and uses them almost nowhere. The one place they appeared, the collect popup, was switched off this morning because it interrupted the hunt. So the screens read as dry grey admin panels with a game hidden inside. The fix is not more chrome: promote the cast into the layout. Characters become the faces of items, badges, empty states, and the two reward moments (landing, Score). Everything stays static and in-flow — the art is the layout, never an overlay — so the hunt keeps the calm the owner asked for, and Express stays bare.

Decisions in this proposal

  1. The cast is the visual identity. One art style (the shipped characters), extended by generation — never a second style.
  2. Art is layout, never overlay. No popups, no timed cards, nothing over the store photo. The collect-popup ruling stands.
  3. Celebration moves to Score — the one moment it cannot interrupt anything.
  4. Express stays bare. Rush mode gets no art, no faces, no band.
  5. Store truth stays photographic. Illustrations are posters and portraits; the hunt's view of the real store is never drawn over or replaced.
  6. Restraint rules hold: greyscale structure, orange = the game, light mode only. The one new surface is the characters' own cream (#FBF5E6), used only inside art frames.
  7. Copy stays flat. The fun is in the art and the faces, not in slogans — headlines and labels are the current ones.
