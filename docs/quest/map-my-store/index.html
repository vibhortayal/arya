Map my store ("Teach Arya your store"): design

Me, 2026-10-28: replace "take a photo or record a video" with a guided mapper. Map only the aisles you use, over many visits. Many partial maps merge into one store map. A "not found" or "map is outdated" asks for one specific capture. The engine keeps the best images. Wireframe (v2): docs/quest/map-my-store-wireframe.html. Code: api/storemap.py, assistant/web/src/screens/Mapper.tsx.

Coverage model​

A segment is one side of one aisle, or one wall or freezer run. A store map is its list of segments. Shoppers add segments ("Add an aisle"). No store starts seeded: the old Trader Joe's walkthrough seed was removed (2026-10-29), and a saved section that only had a walkthrough baseline is dropped on load.

Status of a segment, from its best capture:

  • unmapped: no capture, no baseline.
  • mapped: best capture is 60 days old or less and no open flag.
  • aging: best capture older than 60 days.
  • stale: a "not found" or "outdated" flag is newer than the best capture.

Coverage is mapped segments over known segments. The denominator grows as people add aisles, so the bar means "of what is known so far".

Capture prompts​

next_prompt() returns one capture: kind, title, why, numbered steps, time. Order of need: stale, then unmapped, then aging. The shopper's focus ("Mine" aisles, saved per account) is served first. Two or more unmapped segments in one zone get one walk prompt (about 20 s, the phone sends its 6 sharpest of 12 sampled frames as photos; no video upload). Otherwise a photo prompt (about 10 s). A stale prompt names the missing item.

How contributions merge​

One shared file per store (platform plane, like the stores directory). Segments merge on zone_key: "Aisle 4", "aisle #4 - Pasta" are one zone; other labels merge on normalized words, minus a trailing "shelf". Each segment keeps its 3 best captures. Contributors are stored as a hash, never the username. Demo visitors write to their sandbox overlay, never the shared map.

Framing (me, 2026-10-28)​

The shopper teaches Arya so Arya can help them: "Arya knows 64% of Trader Joe's", and every capture says what it unlocks for you (a real shelf photo in your next hunt). Helping other shoppers is the bonus.

Capture: no retakes, ever​

I said: "I click pictures, then app says not good... this will cause friction." So the shopper opens a live camera and the app does the work:

  • Photo: the phone grades every frame (sharpness, light), shows one live hint ("Hold still…", "A bit dark. Face the shelf light."), and snaps the best frame after 1.5 s steady, or the best seen by 6 s.
  • Walk: the sharpest frame of every 2 s is kept; no video upload.
  • The server never refuses a shot. A weak one is kept as a partial (weak: true). The segment stays "aging", the next better capture replaces it, and the engine asks again on a later visit.
  • If the people scrub fails, nothing is kept and the app says "No need to redo it." It never asks for a retake.
  • Without a live camera (old browser, permission denied) the system camera is the fallback, under the same rules.

Splitting a long aisle​

The walk's length decides: about 8 s of walking per section, 1 to 6 sections. The app calls POST /api/storemap/split and spreads the walk's frames over the sections in time order (front / middle / back, or first half / second half). Parts keep the zone key, so sightings and "not found" reports still find them. A split is idempotent, so a second shopper walking the same aisle fills the same sections. Old whole-aisle captures are dropped on split; the walk that caused it fills the parts.

When Arya asks​

  • After a hunt: one skippable card on the score screen ("Got 20 seconds? Teach Arya Aisle 4. +10"). GET /api/storemap/next?items= puts an aisle where something on today's list was seen first. "Not now" mutes the store for 7 days (on the phone).
  • Can't find it: a photo attached to the "Not here" report is ingested straight into the flagged section (no extra screen). Without one, the toast offers "Open camera". An item with no known aisle asks "Which aisle are you in?" as one tap.

Rewards (no pressure)​

+10 for a section that becomes mapped, +3 for a refresh or confirmation. Titles: Scout 1, Pathfinder 5, Cartographer 15, Store whisperer 40 sections. Each capture shows the store going from a% to b% and the sections to the next 25% step. Credit is per account (map_credit.json); nothing is ever taken away, and there are no streaks or timers.

How the engine chooses frames​

All deterministic, $0, except two model calls per upload in the route: SAM 3.1 people scrub (fails closed) and "grocery shelf" boxes for fill (summed over bays, measured on the scrubbed JPEG).

  1. Grade: sharpness (Laplacian variance), exposure (mean grey level), shelf fill. A weak frame is kept as a partial, never refused.
  2. Score = 0.5 sharpness + 0.3 exposure + 0.2 fill (fill is neutral if the check failed).
  3. Value = score x freshness; freshness halves every 30 days.
  4. Same view as a kept capture (dhash distance 6 or less) is a confirmation: it refreshes the old capture's date, and swaps the image only if clearly sharper (+0.05). That clears a stale flag.
  5. Keep the top 3 by value. The best one is shown for that segment.

Refresh loop​

POST /api/storemap/outdated or a "Not here" report marks a segment stale. For an item, the segment is where the item was last seen (note_sighting from Find it for me). If unknown, the item is parked as unplaced and the shopper is asked which aisle they are in. The report response carries map_task; the app offers "Take the photo".

Privacy​

Only scrubbed frames are kept. No opt-in screens yet (deferred by me for the hackathon). Raw frames never touch disk.

Not built yet​

Item-level locations from captures (Muse Spark labels); automatic resolved transitions for reports; stale flags expiring when no one confirms; mapping silently from Find it for me frames during a hunt.
