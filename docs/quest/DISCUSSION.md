# Discussion log — how Arya Quest got its shape

The owner's steering, in order, 2026-10-27. Each entry ends with what it changed.

1. **A 3D store, for fun.** "Build a 3D model of the grocery store from pictures and videos I take — walk through it, it shows you we are here, go next, go next." → The store layer stopped being a camera-overlay afterthought and became a place you walk. (Stage 5 reframed.)
2. **Compliance check.** "Does it comply with the hackathon guidelines and use Meta products?" → Rule set: the 3D is the window, Meta models are the builder. Rendering/plumbing may be commodity; perception, reconstruction, and judgment must be Meta.
3. **A Meta model for the 3D itself.** Owner asked for a Meta product to build 3D from video; he supplies walkthrough videos (a Trader Joe's tour first). → **VGGT / VGGT-Ω** (Meta AI + Oxford) does geometry; SAM 3.1 does products; Spark does labels. Blender demoted to optional cleanup. Spike: 90 frames from a 90-second HD aisle stretch, reconstruction run same day.
4. **The pivot: make shopping fun.** List in any form (handwritten note photo, whiteboard, paste) → pick the store → unmapped stores get mapped by their own shoppers filming aisles → next visit, you *play* the shop. Game skins over one mechanic: treasure hunt, escape room. → The product's face became the game; Arya's engine (patterns, receipts, ledger) moved underneath as the brain.
5. **The receipt ends every shop.** "At the end we always capture the receipt — that feeds our eventual goal." → The receipt is the finish ritual, the scorecard, and the verified data source in one act.
6. **Platform vs account.** Modeling and store uploads live at **platform level**, shared by all players of a store. Lists, receipts, parsing, and personal analysis live at **account level**, private. → The two-plane architecture in ARCHITECTURE.md.
7. **The Pokémon GO frame.** Catching characters in the real world is the engagement model — personal spawns, viral shape. → Pitch's one-liner: "Pokémon GO for your grocery list."
8. **Kids are the sharp end.** Kids cry and scatter in stores; a hunting kid follows the list exactly. → Parents-first audience; the family case is a design center, not an edge case.
9. **Modes are moods, not ages.** "Sometimes you are in a rush, sometimes in the fun mood — don't call it kids mode and parent mode." → **Quest mode** (play) and **Express mode** (rush), same model and list.
10. **Wireframes before building.** Easy, intuitive, simple. His words: tap → the list appears; check things off → the list goes away; multiple stores in a day = different games, sessions on the side. → UX.md (session model, five list-panel rules) + a 10-screen clickable wireframe browser. **Approved by the owner the same evening** — the build gate is open.
11. **Community layer.** Leaderboards, funny verified badges, teams racing live, game-show clue challenges. → GAME-DESIGN.md "Community layer": receipt-verified boards, the badge catalog (Egg Baron … Return Regret), Shop-Offs, Split the List, Clue Hunt. Docs and pitch now; score-screen badges in the demo; full build post-hackathon.

12. **Shop-Off ships.** "Shop-off needs to be there — the community section is the clincher for hackathon virality." → Community promoted from rollout to demo-grade build scope: playable Shop-Off, household board, live badges.
13. **Friends and Profile ship.** "Add friends, profile — this needs to be there." → Handle-based friends with challenge shortcuts; profile as the public card (stats, badge shelf, mapper credit; private data never shown). Wireframe screens 12–13.
14. **Muse writes the list.** "Need to have Muse connector to put items on grocery list too." → The connector (already live on the engine) becomes session-aware: chat adds land in today's session for the named store. Fifth intake door, added to the wireframes' Add-list screen.

15. **Profile holds the receipts.** "Profile can save receipt photos and have option to see digitized the list as well. Future part is analysis dashboard etc." → Profile = public card + private receipt archive (photo with digitized twin); analysis/dashboard declared as the future layer, shown in the UI as a named placeholder only.

16. **A smart optimizer is step one.** "If I add milk three times it should update the quantity… if I put a spelling mistake it should correct it or ask me… I might buy organic blueberries AND frozen blueberries — it should reason it out, or ask me." → The Optimizer (ARCHITECTURE §2b): merge → correct (shown, with undo) → ask on real ambiguity; runs after store pick, before mode choose, on lists from all five doors; unanswered questions block the start.
17. **Drift patrol, paid on truth.** "We also need a way for users to capture drift in store design… Users should be rewarded for this?" — and the stockout objection, raised by the owner himself: a missing item may be moved, or merely out of stock. → The Spotter economy (GAME-DESIGN.md): "Not here" with Empty shelf / Something else here / Can't tell + a shelf photo (the tag is the tell); reports flag, time resolves (a later collect at the modeled stop = stockout; an independent confirm at a new spot = the move applies); points land only on resolution — 2 for a true stockout, 10 for a verified move — plus the Eagle Eye badge and per-store Guardian credit, all status, never cash. Owner's capstone: "This builds real-time community-driven inventory!" — every hunt is a shelf survey, so the platform knows what a store holds right now, and stockouts feed pre-trip warnings.
18. **Legal watchlist.** "Is there any legal pitfalls I should be aware of for this?" → a watchlist was written (filming on private property, bystanders in footage, COPPA, VGGT's non-commercial weights, CPRA-sensitive receipts). Nothing on it blocks the hackathon. The document itself was later moved out of this repo (entry 29).
19. **Playable without the 3D.** "Make the game still playable without the 3D render?" → The scene ladder (UX.md): Cloud / Aisle / Strip drivers render one session identically — stops, COLLECT, scoring, and the receipt finish never depend on the renderer. Unmapped stores play in Strip by default; mapping becomes an invitation ("unlock the 3D view"), never a gate. A mid-hunt toggle drops a level for battery, comfort, or motion sensitivity. This also removes the launch cold-start problem: thousands of unmapped stores are playable on day one.
20. **The brain matches the game.** "Brain Arya's wireframes and UX needs to match the Quest ones. I like the Quest ones. Can we simplify Brain Arya's UI UX and build some wireframes before you develop?" → Brain wireframes join the same artifact in Quest's visual language: List (with a plain "why" per item), Add (five doors, Muse first), Ask, Receipts (review queue folded in as "Needs your eye" rows), Spend demoted from a tab to a card, and ONE unified Profile for both layers. Bottom bar: Today / List / Add / Ask / Profile — Quest lives as cards on Today, no Quest tab, no Dashboard tab. Process holds: wireframes first, his approval, then development.
21. **The thesis, in his words + the business.** Owner, excited: "Few things that I like: 1. Gamification — makes chore fun. 2. Like Google Maps, but for a grocery store. 3. Community based inventory reports. 4. It also helps keep track of grocery list, it's a smart grocery list maker (there are many, but how many make it fun?)" — then: "Marketing, promotions, monetization can be driven once we have virality." → BUSINESS.md: the four pillars; the three owned moments (list → shelf → receipt) as the position money follows; revenue in unlock order (retailer inventory data → shelf promotions per verified purchase → Arya Plus → platform licensing); density-beats-coverage and never-sell-honesty rules; competitive scan (Fetch/Ibotta, Shopkick shut down, Aisle411, first-party retailer apps, Cooklist, Simbe/Trax — every piece exists, the combination nowhere).
22. **One list, store lens.** "List should be per grocery store?" → Decided against separate lists: the need is household-level and patterns are store-blind, so the brain keeps ONE list; each item carries a store affinity learned from its receipts (the dictionary already tracks store-varying prices), and the List screen gains store chips (All / Costco / Trader Joe's) that lens the same items — store slice first, "could grab here too" below, check-off global, never duplicated. Hunts seed from the store's slice. Shown on the B1 wireframe in three switchable states per the owner's "yes, show it to me".
23. **Never charge the consumer.** Owner, correcting a draft that put analysis behind a paid "Plus" tier: "No — paid brain doesn't analyse. We never charge the consumer. Money will come from other sources, never the consumer." → BUSINESS.md amended: the consumer tier is deleted; the rule is stated as a principle (players are the supply; businesses pay). Analysis is a free product feature — Spend live, Patterns/Trends coming — and the wireframes carry no paid or premium language anywhere. Revenue stands at: retailer inventory data, brand promotions per verified purchase (rebate matching included), platform licensing.
24. **Friend run notifications.** "Can we notify friends of grocery runs?" → Yes (owner 👍): a run-start ping carries the store and the moment, never the list. Three structured actions, no chat: ⚔️ Challenge (the run is Shop-Off's starting gun), ➕ Add one thing (one item + qty as a request; the runner accepts/declines; accepted items file as bought-for-others — ledger yes, patterns never), 👁 Follow (progress count only). Household tier is warmer: direct list adds until the receipt closes the run. One ping per run, per-friend mute, mutual-accept friends only. Recipients are the owner's choice at two levels (his refinement): a standing notify set (bell toggle per friend in Friends) plus per-run chips at run start — set pre-selected, tap to add/drop for this run only. Household pings stay a separate tier (Profile-controlled, not per-run). On paper as panel 13b; engine work joins Stage C.
25. **One repo.** "Why is arya and arya quest 2 different repo?" — they were sequence, not architecture: arya (the product) came first; arya-quest was spun up at the pivot as a clean doc pack. Owner's call: "Merge the doc pack into the arya repo." → This doc pack now lives at vibhortayal/arya under docs/quest/; vibhortayal/arya-quest is archived with a pointer README. One product, one repo. (This entry is the last one written in the old home; the log continues in arya.)
26. **Intake doors — the invariant is Meta, not Muse-only.** Owner, reviewing the wireframes: "Intake is inconsistent. Profile says 'Muse is the only way data enters Arya', but the Add tab shows five doors. I'd keep the in-app photo and voice doors in the demo account, since they would call the Meta API on camera. Voice should use Muse Voice Transcribe." → Profile's Muse-only claim is deleted; the invariant is restated as "every door runs on Meta models — Muse in chat, Spark on the Meta API in the app." Photo stays an in-app door (server decode via the Meta API — the /decode/photo path already exists); Voice is labeled Muse Voice Transcribe (build stage verifies the exact API surface; the live app's current device Web Speech is superseded by this ruling). This revises the 2026-10-27 "Muse-only intake" distribution lock for the demo account.
27. **Wireframes approved; build order set.** Owner: "wireframes approved for now. lets build and test it?" → The final wireframe set (15 Quest screens + Brain B1–B6 + panel 13b) is APPROVED as the build target. Build order: Intake parity first (already running — functional doors/voice in the same files), then the Brain restyle to B1–B6 on top of it, then test (suite + per-screen checks against the wireframes) and deploy for the owner's phone pass.
28. **Repo goes public — closer to the hackathon.** Owner, after Claude's critique pressed for Oct 29: "we will go public, but closer to hackathon." → The repo stays private through the build; the public flip lands with the submission freeze (Spark's recommendation: Nov 3). Pre-flip checklist: scrub VM IP / token locations from STATUS, re-grep the tree for secrets, one-story docs in place.
29. **Stores: search, favorites, add with address (owner design fix) + legal doc moved.** Owner: stores must be findable by address or zip; adding a store takes its address; favorite stores get a star and surface first; more stores can be found and added from the available quests. → New stage (Stage E) after the Brain restyle: a platform-level store directory (name, address, zip, mapped/quest status), search by zip/address/name, account-level My Stores with favorites, an add-a-store form (name + address), and the store pick reading favorites first. This supersedes the old held "Stage 5 store add-on." Same turn: the legal watchlist document moved to the research repo (vibhortayal/meta-hackathon-research, project/LEGAL.md) — legal discussion does not live in the submission repo.

## Standing constraints the discussion produced

- No product build before wireframe approval (given 2026-10-27) and no submission without the owner's explicit go-ahead on the complete package.
- The full-3D scope rides on the VGGT spike verdict; hybrid and 2.5D fallbacks are pre-agreed in BUILD-PLAN.md.
- Fun is the data strategy; the receipt is always the finish.
