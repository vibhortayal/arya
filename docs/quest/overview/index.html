Arya Quest: doc pack

Quest is the optional game layer of Arya, a grocery app that learns how you shop from your receipts and helps shoppers help each other (see the root README.md). Team Nightshift, Indie track, submission Nov 4, 2026, 9:00 PM ET.

Quest turns your grocery list into a treasure hunt in your real store. Pick the store. Arya sorts your list by where you usually buy each thing. Walk the hunt through real photos of the aisles and grab each item (points only go up, no clock). With Find it for me, you point the camera at the shelf and the item lights up. Snap the receipt at checkout. It banks your points and teaches Arya your household, so next week's list writes itself.

An unmapped store still plays, as a labelled typical layout. A guided camera scan maps it for every shopper after you.

Loop​

Plan → Find → Grab → Receipt.

  1. Plan. The list arrives in any form (photo, paste, typing, voice, Muse chat). Pick the store. The list is sorted for it from your receipts.
  2. Find. Shoppers' photos where a store section is mapped (people pixelated by SAM 3.1), and a labelled typical layout elsewhere. Find it for me: SAM 3.1 boxes candidates, and Muse Spark verifies them.
  3. Grab. +10 per grab, milestones, a full-haul bonus. Shortage reports and sightings feed a live store map.
  4. Receipt. Muse Spark reads it (Spark first; see the model map in JUDGES.md). Code checks the arithmetic. The receipt then feeds patterns, spend and returns.
