Your grocery list, as a treasure hunt in your real store.

Arya learns what your household buys from its receipts and writes the list. Quest turns the list into a hunt through the real store: you walk it in photos from one shopper's walkthrough video, with a 3D model of the aisle one tap away. The receipt at checkout settles the score.

Built on Meta models: SAM 3.1 finds the shelves, Muse Spark reads lists and labels the shelves, Muse Wonder reads receipt photos, Muse Voice Transcribe takes dictation, and Muse Image drew the characters that cheer each grab.

Opens a fictional household, no account needed. Your hunts, list changes and chats last only for this visit. The camera and microphone call the Meta API live.

Connect Muse

Connect your Muse — the only setup Arya needs.

  1. Create a token below. It is shown once.
  2. Open Muse and add the Arya connector — or just tell Muse: “connect Arya”.
  3. Paste the token into Muse’s secure form. Never paste it into a chat message.
  4. Tap Done. From then on, talk to Muse — or add to your list on the List tab.

Using a different assistant app? Copy the setup prompt and paste it there once — it teaches the assistant its job. The connector route above stays the better path.

    How to play · 3 steps
    1. Find itThe orange card shows your next item and its shelf; the picture shows where it is in the store. Get any item in any order — the route is just a suggestion.
    2. Grab itIn your cart? Tap Grab: +10 points. Changed your mind? Put back. Shelf empty? Tap “Not here?” and warn the next shopper.
    3. Snap the receiptTap Finish and photograph the receipt: +50, and your points bank. No clock, no way to lose.
