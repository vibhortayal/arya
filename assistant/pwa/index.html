How it works

Arya learns what your household buys from its receipts and keeps your lists. It tells you what is running low and why, what is out or cheaper at your store, and what to use soon.

When you want it fun, Quest turns the list into a hunt through the real store: you walk it in photos from one shopper's walkthrough video, with a 3D model of the aisle one tap away. The receipt at checkout settles the score.

The demo opens a fictional household, no account needed. Your hunts, list changes and chats last only for this visit. The camera and microphone call the Meta API live.

Built on Meta models. Entry in the Meta AI Developer Hackathon.

    The hunt stays closed. Attaching its receipt settles it, and the “Receipt missing” tag clears everywhere.

    How to play · 3 steps
    1. Find itThe orange card shows your next item and its shelf; the picture shows where it is in the store. Get any item in any order — the route is just a suggestion.
    2. Grab itIn your cart? Tap Grab: +10 points. Changed your mind? Put back. Shelf empty? Tap “Not here?” and warn the next shopper.
    3. Snap the receiptTap Finish and photograph the receipt: +50, and your points bank. No clock, no way to lose.
