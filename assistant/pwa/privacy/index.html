Privacy policy

Last updated November 3, 2026. Arya is run by Vibhor Tayal. Questions: support@teamnightshift.org.

Arya is a grocery list that learns your store. This page says what we keep, why, and who else sees it. It is a plain-language summary, not legal advice.

What we keep

  • Your email address, to sign you in, send your sign-in code, and (if you join the waitlist) tell you when you are invited. If you sign in with Google, Google tells us your email address and that it is verified. We receive nothing else from your Google account.
  • What you put in the app: lists, items, receipts and receipt photos, store details, and the photos you choose to take when mapping a store or using the fridge and pantry features.
  • Basic usage records, such as when you signed in and error logs, to keep the service running and safe.

What we use it for

To run Arya for you: your lists, the receipts you scan, price and store hints, and reminders. We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.

Who else handles it

  • Supabase: sign-in codes and, where switched on, storage of account data.
  • Resend: sends the sign-in and waitlist emails.
  • Kroger: at Kroger-family stores Arya sends Kroger the item name and the store's location to look up the aisle, never your name, email or account. Nothing Kroger returns is saved; your phone keeps it only while your trip is open. Arya is not affiliated with or endorsed by Kroger.
  • Your phone's location: when you tap Check my location before a Quest, or in Map my store, your browser asks first; once you have allowed it, Map my store checks your location by itself when it opens. Arya compares your position with the store on your phone and does not save it; it is kept in memory only until you sign out or close Arya. Store search (Near me, or typing a store name once location is allowed) sends your position through Arya's server to Google Places to find stores close by, and Arya does not save it. Location is never required: you can start a Quest without it and map a store by uploading a video or photos.
  • Google: sign-in with Google, and Places search when you look up a store.
  • AI model providers: when you scan a receipt, take a store photo, or ask Arya a question, the content is sent to an AI model to be read or answered. We use Meta's models. Most of what Arya reads (your lists and list photos, receipt PDFs and pasted receipt text, expiry-date photos, fridge and shelf photos, and your questions to Ask Arya) goes to Muse Spark in Meta's Standard mode. Receipt photos are read by Muse Spark first too; when Arya's checks flag a read, Muse Wonder, also in Meta's Standard mode, re-reads the photo. Do not put anything in Arya you would not want sent to an AI model. People in a store photo are pixelated before it is kept.

Your choices

  • Export your lists any time from the app.
  • Delete your account in Settings. To be sure it is you, we email you a 6-digit code (or ask for your password, if your account has one). That removes your account, lists, receipts and photos from Arya, including our database copy. Backups that still hold them are deleted within 30 days.
  • Or write to support@teamnightshift.org and we will delete your data.

Groups and sharing

If you join a group, everyone in it sees the group's lists and every member's receipts, including receipts filed before joining and their photos. Any member can share the group's invite code. You can leave a group at any time: you keep a copy of the lists and take your own receipts with you.

When you tap "Not here?" on a trip, Arya shares the store, the item and the time with other Arya shoppers. Not your name or any account detail, and not a photo. To count each shopper once, the server keeps a short code that stands for your account; it never leaves the server. Other shoppers see it only once 3 or more shoppers report the same item at the same store, and only if the item is on their list. A report clears after 3 days, sooner when someone finds the item, or when you take it back.

Changes

If this page changes in a way that matters, we will say so in the app. The date at the top is the latest update.
