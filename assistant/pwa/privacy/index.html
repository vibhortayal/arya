Arya

Privacy policy

Last updated October 30, 2026. Arya is run by Vibhor Tayal. Questions: support@teamnightshift.org.

Arya is a grocery list that learns your store. This page says what we keep, why, and who else sees it. It is a plain-language summary, not legal advice.

What we keep

  • Your email address, to sign you in, send your sign-in code, and (if you join the waitlist) tell you when you are invited. If you sign in with Google, Google tells us your email address and that it is verified. We receive nothing else from your Google account.
  • What you put in the app: lists, items, receipts and receipt photos, store details, and the photos you choose to take when mapping a store or using the fridge and pantry features.
  • Basic usage records, such as when you signed in and error logs, to keep the service running and safe.

What we use it for

To run Arya for you: your lists, the receipts you scan, price and store hints, and reminders. We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.

Who else handles it

  • Supabase: sign-in codes and, where switched on, storage of account data.
  • Resend: sends the sign-in and waitlist emails.
  • Google: sign-in with Google, and Places search when you look up a store.
  • AI model providers: when you scan a receipt, take a store photo, or ask Arya a question, the content is sent to an AI model to be read or answered. We use Meta's models. Photos of people are blurred before a store photo is kept.

Your choices

  • Export your lists any time from the app.
  • Delete your account in Settings. That removes your account, lists, receipts and photos from Arya.
  • Or write to support@teamnightshift.org and we will delete your data.

Groups and sharing

If you join a group, the other members can see the lists and receipts you share with that group. You can leave a group at any time.

Changes

If this page changes in a way that matters, we will say so in the app. The date at the top is the latest update.
