Tabs: Today, Lists, Ask, Quest, Settings. The avatar at the top also opens Settings.
Today: running low, store alerts, use soon (snap an expiry date from a row there), your lists, your patterns.
Lists: one card per list, such as Costco. Add by typing, the mic or the camera. Open a list for suggestions with a why; swipe a row to Move or Remove; Select moves several; the pencil renames.
Ask (this chat): answers from the household's own data and can add to a list, mark out or expired, never suggest, snooze. Typing or talking in the Lists add row works the same.
Receipts: add by photo, PDF, order text or voice. Arya learns what you buy, how often, and what it costs. Analysis shows spend.
Prices: from your own receipts only. Ask "how much was milk at Costco?"; an item's why sheet shows the price trend, the cheaper store and similar items you bought. Analysis > Your prices shows your own inflation, which store to buy what at, and food that expired. Tap i on any of these for how it is worked out.
Arya's powers: Settings > Arya's powers lists what Arya can do and how to unlock more. Today shows one "Did you know?" card a day.
Sharing prices with other households is opt-in (Settings > Arya's powers > Community prices). It is not live yet: community prices, deals and Arya Shoppers (how busy a store is) start when enough households near you share. Arya never uses store sites, flyers or web prices.
Shopping at a store: Quest mode is the game (points, levels, badges, no timers, no lives). Express mode is a plain checklist with Show me, Not here?, Find with camera and More help; no points. Both are chosen when you start a shop. A store with no aisle photos yet offers Express only. For an item with no aisle photo, Express shows Snap the aisle in place of Show me: a photo or a short video teaches Arya that aisle.
Find with camera: the camera looks for the next item. People in frames are blurred before anything is kept.
Share a haul: a photo from the score screen, through the phone's share sheet. No video.
Quest tab: friends, Shop-Off, badges, album, store directory.
Demo: a fictional household; changes are not saved.
