Tabs: Today, Lists, Ask, Quest, Help, Settings.
Arya is a grocery list that learns what the household buys, so the list writes itself. The list is the main part. Quest is optional.
Arya sends no order and makes no payment. Prices come only from the household's own receipts and from Arya shoppers who choose to share. Arya never uses store sites, flyers or web prices.
A new account has few cards on Today until the first receipt is added. The demo is a fictional household; changes are not saved.
## help | help tab guide tour tutorial lost confused learn started begin
Help tab: tap Help in the tab bar. It has an ask box for how-to questions, three first steps (add a receipt, add an item to a list, ask a question) and a topic for each part of the app with numbered steps and a button that opens the feature.
At the bottom of Help: Read the full guide, Arya's powers, Take the tour again and Send feedback.
Each topic has "Still stuck? Ask Arya", which sends the question to the Ask tab.
## lists | list lists store add item type voice mic camera paper tick circle move remove select rename suggestion suggested new
Lists tab: one list for each store, such as Costco. Tap New list and choose a store.
Open a list. Type an item in the box "Add item here...". Tap the microphone to say it. Tap the camera to read a paper list.
Tap the circle to tick an item off as you shop. Tap the three dots to move an item to another list or remove it. Tap Select to act on several items. Tap the pencil to rename a list.
Under "Suggested for ..." Arya shows items that are due. Tap one to see why. Tap Add to put it on the list.
Tap the three dots at the top of a list for Share, export or import, and Tidy up with Arya.
## running low | running low due suggest suggestion pattern often about to run out why
Running low: Today tab, card "Running low", and the suggestions under each list. Arya uses how often the household buys an item and the last date.
Tap an item to see why it is suggested. Tap Add to put it on the list. Arya needs a buying history, so add receipts first.
Say "we finished the yogurt" in Ask to mark an item as out. Say "never suggest X" to stop a suggestion.
## use soon | use soon expire expiry date goes bad waste food fresh dated photo remind
Use soon: Arya tracks expiry dates and reminds you to use food in time.
After a first receipt, Today asks you to add dates. At any time open Settings, Arya's powers, Use soon, Add a date.
Tap Take a photo of the date on the pack. Check the item and the date. Tap Looks right, track it.
Today shows a card "Use soon" and a "Still good?" check. Settings, Arya's powers, Food waste shows what expired food cost this month.
## receipts | receipt receipts photo pdf scan total return refund recall add history
Receipts: open Settings, Receipts, or tap Add receipt after a trip. You can take a photo, pick a PDF, paste order text or say it.
Arya reads the receipt and checks that the total is right. It keeps every receipt, so you can open one for a return or a recall refund.
A return is taken off the spending. Only you and your group see your receipts.
## spending | spending spend analysis month store budget money pay paid
Spending: open Settings, Spending, or tap Details on the Spending card on Today. It shows spending by month, by store and by item.
Ask "What did I spend in October?" for one month. Arya adds up the receipts with code, not by guessing.
## prices | price prices cheaper cheapest compare expensive trend inflation community deals pricier similar ounce watch
Cheaper elsewhere and Price watch are cards on Today. They show when an item is cheaper at another store, or has gone up 5% or more.
Open Settings, Arya's powers for Compare your stores, Price trends, Similar items (price for each ounce) and Buy where it's cheaper.
You need receipts from two stores to compare. Ask "Where is milk cheaper?" for a quick answer.
Community prices are opt-in: Settings, Arya's powers, Community prices, Turn on. They show prices other Arya shoppers paid. They start when enough households near you share.
## shopping | start shopping shop trip quest express game points badges level streak walk find camera aisle practice map
Start shopping: open a list and tap Start shopping. Settings, "When I start shopping", chooses what opens: Start a Quest, Just my list, or Ask me each time.
Or tap "Or just tick items as I shop" and tick items with no game.
Quest is a game: a 3D model of the store, points, levels, badges, no timers and no lives. Open the Quest tab and tap Start a quest, or Practice.
Find it for me: point the camera at a shelf and Arya finds the next item. People in frames are blurred before anything is kept.
Quest progress (Quest tab) shows quests, badges and mapped stores. Your trips and receipts shows finished trips and friends.
## map my store | map my store scan aisle layout walk video photos mapped build dream
Map my store: open the Quest tab and tap Map my store, or tap the Map my store chip on Ask.
Choose the store. Walk the aisles with the camera and tap Done when you finish. Arya builds the map in the background and shows a "map ready" card.
If there is no signal, frames save on the phone and send later. Arya then walks you to each item in aisle order.
Build (Quest tab) lets you shape a store in the game. The Quest tab Edit button hides what you do not use.
## news | news store news alert alerts out of stock report neighbors shoppers busy quiet heads-up
Store news: Today tab, card "Near you today", and a heads-up at the top of a list. It shows what Arya shoppers report: out of stock, back in stock, quiet times.
To report an empty shelf, find "Report something yourself" on Today and tap Out of stock.
In the demo this news is simulated.
## ask | ask chat talk voice photo fridge question speak answer language spanish hindi
Ask tab: type a question or tap Talk to speak. Arya answers from the household's own lists and receipts. It does not speak aloud.
Tap Photo to send a picture. Choose Add to list, File receipt, Check my fridge or Read the date. A photo changes nothing unless you pick an action.
Ask can add to a list, mark out or expired, never suggest and snooze. Tap Edit on Ask to change the quick questions.
Settings, Preferences, language changes the answer language: English, Spanish or Hindi.
## sharing | share group household invite code friends haul sync together partner family
Share lists with a household: open Settings, tap Start a group and give it a name. Tap Invite someone and send the code. Everyone sees the same lists and receipts. Leaving a group has clear rules for what you keep.
Friends: Quest tab, Your trips and receipts, Friends. Add a friend by username.
Share a haul: after a trip, share a photo of what you bought from the score screen. It is a photo only.
Share a list as a checklist, or export or import one: the three dots on a list, Share, export or import.
## customize | edit customize today card cards hide reorder resize library display text size zoom spacing
Change Today: tap Edit on Today, or touch and hold a card. Drag to reorder, tap the red minus to hide, tap the green plus to add from the card library, choose a card size, or tap Reset.
Lists, Ask and Quest have an Edit button too. Cards with nothing to say stay hidden until they do.
Settings, Display changes text size, spacing, bold text, pictures and motion.
## settings | settings account currency language export delete data powers help guide tour feedback agent connector usage cost stores sign in invite waitlist demo
Settings holds: Stores, Receipts, Spending, Help and guide, Arya's powers, Display, Keeping Arya running, Preferences (currency, language, When I start shopping), Send feedback, Use Arya in another AI app, About, Export my data and Delete my account.
Help and guide: fifteen cards that show how each part works, in the order a new user needs them. The last card replays the tour. Arya's powers lists what Arya does now and what you can turn on; tap i for how a power works.
Keeping Arya running shows what the AI reading costs and the share for all Arya.
Sign-in is by invitation for now. On the landing page tap Join the waitlist. Tap Try it now for the demo; tap Finish demo to leave it.
Use Arya in another AI app lets you add to your list from an AI app you already use.
