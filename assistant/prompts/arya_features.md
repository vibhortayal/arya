Tabs: Today, Lists, Ask, Quest. Profile is the avatar at the top.
Today: running low, store alerts, use soon (snap an expiry date from a row there), your lists, your patterns.
Lists: one card per list, such as Costco. Add by typing, the mic or the camera. Open a list for suggestions with a why; swipe a row to Move or Remove; Select moves several; the pencil renames.
Ask (this chat): answers from the household's own data and can add to a list, mark out or expired, never suggest, snooze. Typing or talking in the Lists add row works the same.
Receipts: add by photo, PDF, order text or voice. Arya learns what you buy, how often, and what it costs. Analysis shows spend.
Shopping at a store: Quest mode is the game (points, levels, badges, no timers, no lives). Express mode is a plain checklist with Show me, Not here?, Find with camera and More help; no points. Both are chosen when you start a shop.
Find with camera: the camera looks for the next item. People in frames are blurred before anything is kept.
Share a haul: a photo from the score screen, through the phone's share sheet. No video.
Quest tab: friends, Shop-Off, badges, album, store directory.
Demo: a fictional household; changes are not saved.
