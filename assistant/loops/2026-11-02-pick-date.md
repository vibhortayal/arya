# 2026-11-02 "Pick date" stays open and refuses past dates

Owner phone test, 2026-11-02: tapping "Pick date" picked today's date and closed at once, and a past date could be picked.

Where: only two date inputs set a use-by date, both in `web/src/screens/Today.tsx`. The receipt date filter (Receipts) needs past dates and is unchanged.
1. The "Use-by dates" sheet after a receipt (`PerishSheet`), the "Pick date" button. This is the one the owner hit.
2. The expiry form's date row (`ConfirmForm`, `#ex-date`): Use soon's type-it or fix-it form, and the confirm step after a photo read.

Cause (1): the date input sits invisibly over the "Pick date" pill. On iPhone the wheel fills in today as it opens, which fired onChange. The row then swapped "Pick date" for a "Use by" pill rendered as a different element, so the input under the open wheel was removed and the wheel closed on today. Also, iPhone does not enforce `min`.

Fix:
- (1) One date input per row, kept mounted before and after a date is set. Only the face changes, so the wheel stays open while you spin it.
- Both: a past date is refused with "That date has passed. Pick today or a later date." (en.json `today.datePast`), and `min` is today.
- A photo read that prefills a past date is left as read, since the user didn't pick it.

Tests: `web/src/screens/pickdate.test.tsx` (3). All fail on main.
