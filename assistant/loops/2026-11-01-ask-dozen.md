# 2026-11-01 Ask: "a dozen eggs" is one carton

Tester on live a36b8ab: Ask "add a dozen eggs" gave "Pasture Eggs 12 ct ×12". `chat_service.pack_size` reads a pack count from the matched item's name (12 ct, 18 count, 6 pk, dozen); `_packs` turns a quantity that is the pack size (or a whole number of packs) into packages: 12 of a 12-count carton is 1, 24 is 2; ounces and gallons are not counts. The prompt says qty counts packages.
