# 2026-10-29 — Start shopping bar on the list

**Ask (owner, issue 1).** Once the list is ready, the shopper gets a
Start shopping option, then chooses Quest or Express. Placement matches
the approved Express wireframe (https://claude.ai/artifact/6Vhf3TWkpRpW65WnTBmcAN):
one bar above the tab bar.

**What changed.** The quiet "Shopping now? Start a hunt" line at the
bottom of a list became a bar above the tabs, "Start shopping · N
items", shown whenever the open list has things to get (hidden while
selecting). A named list plans from its own items; Everything else
plans from the whole list. The flow after the tap is unchanged: store,
plan, then the Quest or Express chooser. SW arya-v81.
