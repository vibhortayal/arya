# 2026-10-30 — pypdf 5.9 to 6.21.4

From the 2026-10-30 audit (owner approved H3).

- pypdf 5.9.0 had about 60 advisories: crafted PDFs that loop forever or
  use all memory. Any signed-in account can send a PDF (`/decode/pdf`,
  receipt import); the demo cannot.
- `api/requirements.txt`: `pypdf>=6.21.4,<7`. The app uses only
  `PdfReader(...).pages` and `page.extract_text()`
  (`api/decode/service.py`), which did not change. `pip-audit` now
  lists no pypdf advisory.
- Checked: `tests/fixtures/receipt.pdf` gives the same 126 characters on
  5.9.0 and 6.21.4. `test_decode`, `test_receipt_media`,
  `test_intake_parity`, `test_receipt_dedupe` and `test_demo`: 50 tests,
  green on both versions.
- The deploy image picks it up on the next rebuild (`Dockerfile.api`
  installs `requirements.txt`).
