  • What is Arya. A grocery app that learns how you shop from your receipts and helps shoppers help each other. Read the idea
  • Why I built it. I had this problem: carrying the list in my head, forgetting what was running out, not knowing where the money went. Arya frees up that time and mental space. It is for shoppers, and not for money: households and groups share lists and help each other. Principles
  • What it uses. Meta's models: Muse Spark, Muse Wonder, SAM 3.1, Muse Voice Transcribe, Muse Image and Muse Spark Open, each with one job. Model map
